## Final prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create one ultra-photorealistic premium fitness campaign photograph for a black-and-crimson gym website. A powerful athletic adult performing a heavy barbell deadlift inside a dark luxury gym, dramatic side profile, realistic athletic physique, black training apparel, subtle sweat and natural skin texture. Correct neutral spine deadlift setup, natural hands gripping a physically believable straight Olympic bar with black plates on both ends. Deep black environment, crimson-red rim lighting outlining athlete and barbell from right behind, soft white side light, restrained volumetric haze, realistic anatomy, high-end sports advertising photography, cinematic contrast. Wide horizontal 16:9 composition, athlete predominantly on LEFT half and clean very dark negative space on RIGHT half. Full body, barbell and plates in frame. No text, logos, watermark, branding, plastic skin or illustration.
